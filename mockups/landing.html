Privacy

Designed to collect as little as possible.

  • There are no student accounts, no emails and no Google sign-in.
  • Students use a nickname or tap their initials. With a class list, full names stay in your own browser and are never sent to the server.
  • Drawings exist only while a class is active. A class that has been idle for 6 hours is deleted from the server.
  • Teams, scores and question sets are saved in your browser. Use Back up everything to keep a copy.

Check with your school

Every school has its own approval process. Please check with your school’s data-privacy contact before using any tool with students.
